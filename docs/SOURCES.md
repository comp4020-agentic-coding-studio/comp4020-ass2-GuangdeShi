## Whole-course source pack (Parts II–IV research pass)

**Purpose and method.** Before writing Chapters 4–12, this pass verified a
larger set of authoritative sources so future chapters are built on
evidence that has actually been read, not assumed from a title. Each
source below was opened and read (directly, or by a background research
agent using WebFetch/WebSearch on my behalf) — never registered on title
alone. Four of the originally-supplied URLs turned out to already be
registered from the Chapters 1–3 pass: the **2013 AAFP/ISFM Environmental
Needs Guidelines is `S1`**, the **2021 AAHA/AAFP Feline Life Stage
Guidelines is `S2`**, the **2022 AAFP/ISFM Cat Friendly Veterinary
Interaction Guidelines is `S3`**, and the **2014 AAFP/ISFM House-Soiling
Guidelines is `S4`** — see "Re-verification of S1–S4" below for what this
pass added to them. Everything else is newly registered as `S8`–`S21`.

**Access-honesty note, stated once here rather than repeated on every
entry:** `aaha.org` and `journals.sagepub.com` blocked automated fetching
(HTTP 403) on every URL tried, across multiple independent attempts. Where
a source lives behind one of those domains, this register says so plainly
and reports only what was actually read (a PMC mirror, a `catvets.com`
landing page, or citation metadata plus corroborating secondary
commentary) rather than presenting unread content as verified. No teaching
claim should be built on a detail that is flagged below as unread.
