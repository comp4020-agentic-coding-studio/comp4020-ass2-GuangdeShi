# Policies and support

## Learning scope

SLOP5251 teaches observation, interpretation and safe owner-level decision-making. It does not
provide veterinary diagnosis, prescribe treatment or replace professional care. A real concern
about a cat's health should be taken to a veterinarian; urgent signs should not wait for a course
reply or classroom discussion.

## Assessment support

Each formal brief states its due date, deliverable and expectations. Chapter Checks are formative
and carry no assessment weight. Ask for clarification early when the scope of a task is unclear.
Use the host institution's formal extension and special-consideration processes when circumstances
affect a deadline; this fictional course site does not override those rules.

## Academic integrity and assistance

Submitted reasoning must be your own and sources must be acknowledged. Editing, accessibility and
software assistance should be declared when it materially shapes the work. A tool may help arrange
a case record; it cannot supply the observation or judgement you claim as yours.

## Access and participation

Cat ownership, animal handling and disclosure of a personal health situation are never required.
Equivalent supplied cases are available for observation tasks. Contact the convenor privately when
you need an accessible format or another way to participate in an activity.
