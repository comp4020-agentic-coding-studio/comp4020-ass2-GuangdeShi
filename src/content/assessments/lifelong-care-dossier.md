## Purpose

The capstone asks whether the course has changed the way you think across time. A plan that suited
a kitten may become unhelpful, inaccessible or impossible thirteen years later. Good care keeps its
commitment while changing its form.

## The brief

> Show how one defensible care decision must change when the cat, the home and the caregiver no
> longer match their earlier selves.

Work from the supplied longitudinal case archive. Identify the old baseline, the new pattern, the
decision that now needs revisiting, and the evidence that makes revision necessary.

## What you submit

Submit one coherent dossier: baseline and change record, revised judgement, adaptive care plan,
and a short brief for the next professional conversation. The pieces should work together as one
record rather than as separate answers to twelve chapters.

## Basic expectations

Strong work carries earlier learning forward without flattening a very old cat into a checklist. It
shows what the caregiver can observe and change, what requires veterinary expertise, and how a
sustainable plan includes the household's real capacity.

## Timing

Due at the end of the course. The final fortnight is reserved for integration, revision and checking
that the dossier remains useful to a person who did not watch you make it.
