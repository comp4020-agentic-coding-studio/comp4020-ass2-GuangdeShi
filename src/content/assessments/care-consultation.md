## Purpose

This is the course method under pressure. The case contains enough information to act, but not
enough to erase uncertainty. Your work should help an ordinary caregiver make the next decision
without pretending to replace a veterinarian.

## The brief

> Turn a worried household's story into observations, comparisons and one defensible next step.

Choose one supplied case file. Begin with what is visible, reconstruct the cat's baseline, and test
the household's preferred explanation against at least one credible alternative.

## What you submit

Submit a concise consultation record designed to be used: an observation brief, an evidence map,
a comparison of options, and a care recommendation written for the household. Include what would
change the recommendation and what information should be taken to a veterinary conversation.

## Basic expectations

The strongest response is not the most certain one. It makes uncertainty legible, preserves the
limits of its sources, and chooses a proportionate owner-level action. It should be possible for a
reader to disagree with your first interpretation while still following your reasoning.

## Timing

Due after Capture 08. By this point you have practised body-language interpretation, needs-based
environmental response, baseline comparison, preventive routines and household-level risk
decisions.
