## The recurring movement

A Capture begins with a moment involving Milo. You record what can actually be observed, make an
initial judgement, and then work through explanation, evidence and applied cases. The original
problem returns only after you have enough information to revise the decision and make a practical
Care Plan.

The order matters. Evidence appears before certainty, and every recommendation keeps the boundary
between ordinary owner action and professional veterinary help visible.

## How to use the field notebook

Save concrete observations and reasoning as you work. These notes stay on your device and are not
submitted automatically. The formal assignments ask you to select, revise and explain the evidence
that genuinely changed your judgement.
