# The First 72 Hours

**SLOP6251 · Capture 01**

Week 01 · 22 February 2027

A briefing before the consultation.

---

## Minute six

The carrier door is open.

Milo is under the bed.

You hear one small movement, then nothing.

**What can this moment actually tell you?**

---

## Observe before explaining

The visible record is small:

- the carrier is open and empty
- Milo moved beneath the bed within minutes
- she can be heard but is not visible
- the reason is not yet known

Do not let a plausible story become an observation.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

## Hiding is a coping strategy

In a newly arrived cat, withdrawal can reduce stimulation and preserve control over distance.

Pulling Milo out would answer the owner's uncertainty by removing the strategy she is already using.

**CH1-01 · DIRECT**

---

## Build one small first territory

Prepare a quiet room with:

- a defensible hiding place and resting area
- litter separated from food and water
- predictable access to each resource
- space that can expand gradually

**CH1-02 · CH1-03**

---

## Let the carrier become ordinary

Keep it open and familiar inside Milo's safe territory.

It should not appear only before disruptive travel.

**CH1-04 · DIRECT**

---

## Follow Milo's pace

Do not chase, retrieve, pick up, or force contact.

Reduce demands. Let Milo watch, withdraw, and approach when she is ready.

Expand her territory as she settles rather than according to a fixed clock.

**CH1-05 · CH1-06**

---

## Remove hazards before exposure

- remove true lilies from the home
- secure human medication
- use only pest products labelled safe for cats

Known hazards call for prevention, not watchful waiting.

**CH1-07 · DIRECT**

---

## Add professional screening

Arrange prompt veterinary examination, screening, and parasite checks.

Keep a new kitten separate from resident cats until those steps are addressed.

The owner observes and reports. The veterinary team determines clinical action.

**CH1-08 · DIRECT**

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## Return to Milo

Leave accessible resources and a hiding place nearby.

Let her decide when to emerge.

Begin the daily observation log that later Chapters will use as her baseline.
