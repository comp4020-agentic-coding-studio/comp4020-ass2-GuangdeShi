<div className="coping-line" aria-label="Milo keeps control over distance">
  <span>new room</span><i></i><strong>less stimulation</strong><i></i><span>control over distance</span>

<p className="briefing-evidence">CH1-01 · S1 + S2 · DIRECT</p>

```notes
Hiding after arrival is supported as normal coping and stress-reduction behaviour. Keep the claim narrow: it does not prove how long Milo will hide or rule out every future concern. It changes the immediate owner response from retrieval to lower pressure.

CH1-01 is DIRECT from S1 and corroborated by S2.
```

## Some risks should be removed, not watched.

<Chapter01Hazards />

<p className="briefing-evidence">CH1-07 · S6 · DIRECT</p>

```notes
True lilies in the Lilium and Hemerocallis groups can cause life-threatening kidney injury from very small exposures, including pollen. Secure human medication such as ibuprofen and naproxen. Use only flea, tick, or insecticide products labelled safe for cats.

CH1-07 is DIRECT from S6. S6 is a lower evidentiary tier used only for these specific operational hazard facts.
```
