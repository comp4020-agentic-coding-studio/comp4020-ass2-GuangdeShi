# Process reflection

First, I set two expectations for the course based on the assignment brief. It needed to be something people would genuinely be interested in taking, and it also needed enough depth to support twelve weeks while still being unusual enough that a normal university probably would not offer it. I considered several topics before choosing *How to Keep One Cat Happy for Twenty Years*. It connected directly to everyday life, but it also gave me one idea that could develop across a cat’s whole lifetime.

After choosing the topic, I divided course design into different kinds of work. I researched skills that matched each part of the process, including backwards design, learning-path planning, curriculum development and educational review. These skills helped organise the work, while decisions about the course structure and direction stayed with me. The first evidence-backed curriculum foundation is recorded in [`bf2fd214`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-GuangdeShi/commit/bf2fd214).

An important part of building the course was making sure the teaching content had reliable support. Some cat-care suggestions can sound reasonable without having enough veterinary or behavioural evidence behind them. I used feline-care guidelines, veterinary material and behaviour sources to build the knowledge base for the course. Claims were registered against sources, DIRECT claims were separated from SYNTHESIS, and I defined an owner-facing medical boundary: students can observe, record, compare changes and know when to seek professional help, but they are not taught to diagnose. These rules were formalised in [`1056188`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-GuangdeShi/commit/1056188). I then reviewed the proposed twelve-chapter structure and locked the curriculum backbone in [`4f11292`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-GuangdeShi/commit/4f11292).

After the curriculum was stable, I reviewed the writing itself. Some sections were accurate but still felt too smooth, repetitive and similar in rhythm. I created a course voice workflow and added separate narrative, humanisation and evidence-preservation passes. This was recorded in [`5dd1d27`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-GuangdeShi/commit/5dd1d27). “A warm field notebook following one cat across a lifetime” became the main voice rule used to judge whether the writing belonged in the course.

The website implementation started after the course content and voice were stable. Chapter 1 became the reference Capture, with scene, observation, judgement, evidence, Care Plan and Chapter Check. I reviewed this chapter first, then used the same approved structure to build the remaining chapters. The Chapter 1 reference implementation is recorded in [`19bbe1f`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-GuangdeShi/commit/19bbe1f).

After all twelve chapters were implemented, I compared them as a complete course. The audit checked textual similarity, capability overlap and progression between chapters. It showed that the chapters were distinct, but it also exposed several smaller problems. I rebalanced answer positions, corrected Chapter 8 callbacks, reduced repeated teaching of the Chapter 6 method, and clarified the Chapter 7–9 progression. I also researched presentation skills and adapted a lightweight local `capture-deck` skill for the Chapter 1 briefing deck. These changes are recorded in [`15fb6ce`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-GuangdeShi/commit/15fb6ce).

The workflow that developed through the project was consistent: decide what was needed, produce one version, validate it, then record successful rules before repeating the work at a larger scale. Curriculum structure, source quality, writing voice and visual direction remained human decisions. I completed and accepted a content audit covering evidence trace, DIRECT/SYNTHESIS, the medical boundary, learning alignment, scenario validity, transfer, cross-course consistency and assessment alignment. It found and repaired Chapter 2's missing learner-facing S3 mapping for CH2-08 ([`2521d6f`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-GuangdeShi/commit/2521d6f51377bc141f7ea571e5da824ea08a2acf)). Final whole-site QA then covered automated checks, student-facing routes, both marker viewports, keyboard use, resize state, reduced-motion implementation, degraded media and a marker walkthrough ([`4768953`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-GuangdeShi/commit/47689537bd6b5e92d344d151cddfdfd782ff81e0)). It recorded zero blockers, one major and four minor issues. The MAJOR found in final QA was resolved by replacing the unfinished Capture template entry with a student-facing field guide. This left deployment as the final submission step.
